Scientific Sources & Citations

1. Solar Irradiance

Kopp, G. and Lean, J.L. (2011). "A new, lower value of total solar irradiance." Geophysical Research Letters, 38(1), L01706. DOI: 10.1029/2010GL045777
Used for: Solar constant = 1,361 W/m²

2. Earth Gravitational Model (J2)

Pavlis, N.K. et al. (2012). "The development and evaluation of EGM2008." J. Geophys. Res., 117, B04406. DOI: 10.1029/2011JB008916
Used for: J₂ = 1.08263 × 10⁻³ (SSO precession rate)

3. Atmospheric Density (NRLMSISE-00)

Picone, J.M. et al. (2002). "NRLMSISE-00 empirical model of the atmosphere." J. Geophys. Res., 107(A12), 1468. DOI: 10.1029/2002JA009430
Used for: Density lookup tables, drag calculations
Emmert, J.T. et al. (2021). "NRLMSIS 2.0: Whole-atmosphere empirical model." Earth and Space Science, 8(3), e2020EA001321. DOI: 10.1029/2020EA001321
Used for: Updated density model comparison (~10% lower than NRLMSISE-00)

4. Orbital Debris (ORDEM 3.1)

Manis, A. et al. (2022). "NASA ORDEM 3.1 Model Process." NASA/TP-20220004345. NTRS Link
Used for: Debris flux vs altitude (Gaussian peak at ~800 km)
Liou, J.-C. and Johnson, N.L. (2006). "Risks in Space from Orbiting Debris." Science, 311(5759), 340–341. DOI: 10.1126/science.1121337
Used for: Kessler syndrome projections, debris growth multiplier

5. Drag Coefficient

Wertz, J.R. and Larson, W.J. (eds.) (1999). Space Mission Analysis and Design (SMAD), 3rd Ed. Microcosm Press.
Used for: Cd ≈ 2.2 (flat plate, free molecular flow)
Pilinski, M.D. et al. (2011). "Drag Coefficients of Satellites with Flat Plates." J. Spacecraft & Rockets, 48(5). DOI: 10.2514/1.50915
Used for: Cd range 2.0–2.2 validation

6. Solar Cell Efficiency & Degradation

France, R.M. et al. (2022). "Triple-junction solar cells with 39.5% terrestrial and 34.2% space efficiency." Joule, 6, 1121–1135. DOI: 10.1016/j.joule.2022.04.024
Used for: Record AM0 space efficiency (34.2%)
NREL (2025). "Best Research-Cell Efficiency Chart." nrel.gov
Used for: Panel efficiency ranges (opt/base/pess)
Messenger, S.R. et al. (2001). "Modeling solar cell degradation in space." Progress in Photovoltaics, 9(2), 103–121. DOI: 10.1002/pip.357
Used for: Radiation degradation rates (%/yr)

7. Hall Thruster Propulsion

Goebel, D.M. and Katz, I. (2008). Fundamentals of Electric Propulsion: Ion and Hall Thrusters. JPL Space Science & Technology Series. Wiley. PDF
Used for: Hall thruster Isp ≈ 3,000 s
Sutton, G.P. and Biblarz, O. (2017). Rocket Propulsion Elements, 9th Ed. Wiley.
Used for: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation (station-keeping propellant)

8. Orbital Mechanics & SSO

Vallado, D.A. (2013). Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications, 4th Ed. Microcosm Press.
Used for: SSO precession equations, nodal regression
Bussy-Virat, C.D. and Ridley, A.J. (2019). "Computation of Eclipse Time for LEO Small Satellites." Int. J. Aviation, Aeronautics, Aerospace, 6(5). Link
Used for: Eclipse duration calculations, beta angle geometry

9. Solar Cycle & Thermospheric Variability

Tapping, K.F. (2013). "The 10.7 cm solar radio flux (F10.7)." Space Weather, 11, 394–406. DOI: 10.1002/swe.20064
Used for: Solar activity levels (F10.7 proxy)
Hapgood, M.A. et al. (2022). "Unexpected space weather causing Starlink reentry." J. Space Weather and Space Climate, 12. DOI: 10.1051/swsc/2022009
Used for: Real-world drag crisis validation (38 Starlinks lost)

10. Thermal Management

CODATA (2018). "Fundamental Physical Constants." NIST
Used for: Stefan-Boltzmann σ = 5.670374419 × 10⁻⁸ W/m²·K⁴
Gilmore, D.G. (ed.) (2002). Spacecraft Thermal Control Handbook, Vol. 1. The Aerospace Press / AIAA.
Used for: Radiator sizing, heat pipe design, emissivity values
Ewert, M.K. et al. (1997). "Active Heat Rejection System Using Heat Pumps." NASA JSC / SAE 972434.
Used for: Space heat pump COP and mass estimates

11. Launch Vehicle Economics

SpaceX (2020). "Starship Users Guide, Rev. 1.0." spacex.com
Used for: Payload to LEO > 100 t; SSO estimates 50–100 t

12. Atmospheric Limb Attenuation

Chapman, S. (1931). "The absorption and dissociative or ionizing effect of monochromatic radiation." Proc. Phys. Soc., 43(5). DOI: 10.1088/0959-5309/43/5/302
Used for: Chapman function (limb optical path)
Kasten, F. and Young, A.T. (1989). "Revised optical air mass tables and approximation formula." Applied Optics, 28(22), 4735–4738. DOI: 10.1364/AO.28.004735
Used for: Air mass formula for ground comparison
