Panel & Spacecraft

2.2
Drag coefficient for flat plate in free molecular flow. Standard value 2.2 from Sentman (1961). Range 2.0–2.4 depending on surface accommodation. Used by ESA DRAMA, NASA DAS.
0.30
Panel surface reflectivity (specular + diffuse). Affects SRP force: F = P×A×(1+r). Solar cells ~0.1–0.2, cover glass ~0.3, typical array average ~0.3. Source: Montenbruck & Gill (Satellite Orbits), Table 3.4.
1.5
Annual panel efficiency loss from radiation, thermal cycling, UV. Triple-junction GaAs in LEO: ~1–3%/year. 1.5% is moderate for 600–700 km with no eclipse cycling. Source: Spectrolab (ITJ cell data), NASA LILT testing, ESA ECSS-E-ST-20-08C.

Propulsion

1,800
Specific impulse of the Hall effect thruster. Ranges: SPT-100 ~1,600s, BPT-4000 ~2,000s, SPT-140 ~1,770s, Starlink krypton thrusters ~800–1,500s. Higher Isp = less propellant but more power needed. Source: Aerojet Rocketdyne, Fakel, Busek datasheets.
50
Thrust per unit electrical input power. BHT-600: ~50 mN/kW, SPT-100: ~55 mN/kW, BPT-4000: ~60 mN/kW. Lower values for higher-Isp thrusters. Source: Busek, Aerojet Rocketdyne product data.
15%
Fraction of raw SRP acceleration that requires active station-keeping. Most SRP effect in LEO is absorbed by frozen orbit design (J2 precession cancels eccentricity drift). 0% = perfectly frozen orbit. 10–20% = typical estimate for residual inclination/eccentricity trim. This is the LARGEST UNCERTAINTY in the model. Source: Montenbruck & Gill Ch. 3, Milani et al. (1987), mission experience from ESA GOCE, GRACE.

Earth Comparison

20.9%
Annual-average GHI as % of AM0 solar constant (1361 W/m²). Best desert sites (Atacama, Sahara): ~2,500 kWh/m²/yr = 285 W/m² = 20.9%. Includes day/night, atmosphere, clouds. Source: PVGIS (EU JRC), NREL NSRDC, Solargis.
12.6%
Global land average GHI: ~1,500 kWh/m²/yr = 171 W/m² = 12.6% of 1361. Includes all latitudes, cloud cover, seasons. Source: World Bank Global Solar Atlas, NASA POWER.
